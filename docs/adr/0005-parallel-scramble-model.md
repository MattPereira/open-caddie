# Model Scrambles separately from Tournaments and Matches

Scrambles use parallel event, team, and score records while reusing Course and Tee records. Although Scrambles share date and course fields with Tournaments and Matches, generalizing those established models now would require broad migrations without yet-proven shared behavior; isolation gets the real event into use sooner and guarantees Scramble scores cannot enter Seasons, standings, handicaps, or `round_summaries`. A shared Event model can be extracted later if multiple formats prove a useful common interface.

A Scramble explicitly references both its Course and required Tee; a composite foreign key guarantees that Tee belongs to that Course. Its Teams have case-insensitively unique names and a required Starting Hole, while team members are variable-size child records with no User identity or domain-significant ordering. Team Scores are sparse, non-null child records keyed by Team and hole: a row exists only after strokes are recorded.

The Scramble handle is a permanent, globally unique, lowercase public identity. Schedule is stored as a local date and start time plus an IANA timezone. Admins may correct a Scramble's Course, Tee, Team name, roster, or Starting Hole after scoring begins; scores retain their Team and hole identities. A Scramble cannot be deleted while it owns Teams, while deleting a Team cascades to its members and Team Scores. Future application write paths must create every Team transactionally with at least one member, prevent removal of its final member, preserve handle immutability, and validate the timezone against the IANA database.

Member names need not be unique, and the database imposes no Team or roster-size caps. Capability credentials are deferred until their rotation, expiry, and device-session behavior are decided; landing-page storage is likewise deferred until its content model is decided. Neither concern is embedded speculatively in the core Scramble tables.
