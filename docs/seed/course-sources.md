# Seed course data

Reviewed 2026-11-24 for #258. Fictional amateur Players and Clubs play these real Courses; their rounds and results are invented. No real player results are represented.

Course data lives in `src/seed/world/course-data.ts`. Yardages are yards. Use men's pars, stroke indexes and ratings, matching the app's existing single-rating-per-Tee model. Regular tees only; no composite tees or tournament-only setups. Keep these snapshots fixed until deliberately rechecking the sources and the seed scoring scenarios together.

## Source limitations

Mauna Kea's current resort page publishes pars but no complete scorecard. Its hole yardages and stroke indexes use the linked secondary transcription, corroborated against the resort's [published anniversary Pro-Am scorecard](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5300f7cfe4b0056790cce354/t/54c098bde4b04b34af200513/1421908157336/MaunaKeaProAm%2B%28hi-res%29.compressed.pdf). Current HSGA ratings and lengths agree for the three selected tees. The full post-renovation card remains to be confirmed directly with the resort; these are sourced seed snapshots, not a live course-data service.

Bethpage's former official scorecard URL no longer responds. Hole yardages and stroke indexes use the linked secondary transcription; totals and tee names follow the current USGA entry. Older cards call the forward tee Red and list different ratings; this snapshot uses the current Yellow tee.

Royal County Down's displayed White OUT/IN subtotals contain a discrepancy: its individual holes sum to 3,421 + 3,504 = 6,925. Use the individual hole values, which agree with the published 18-hole total and USGA length. Yellow is deliberately omitted because its published total differs between sources.
